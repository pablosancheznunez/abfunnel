# abfunnel 0.1.0

* First release.
* `af_design()`: sample size and duration for A/B and A/B/n tests.
* `af_srm()`: sample ratio mismatch check.
* `af_experiment()` and `af_analyse()`: differences and relative lifts with
  confidence intervals, Holm-adjusted tests and the Bayesian probability of
  beating the control.
* `af_funnel()`: step-by-step funnel analysis with intervals.
* `af_plot_lift()`, `af_plot_funnel()` and `af_report()`: charts and a
  self-contained report.
* `af_preregistration_template()`: Markdown template to pre-register an
  experiment.
* `experiment_demo` and `funnel_demo`: synthetic data with known effects.
